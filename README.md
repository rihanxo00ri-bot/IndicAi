# IndicAi — AI-Assisted Research Paper Intelligence

> An AI-powered research paper intelligence system designed to investigate, organize, and verify information from academic research papers using multiple specialized AI investigators.

**Project Status:** 🚧 Under Development

## 1. Project Overview

Research papers often contain complex terminology, mathematical explanations, experimental results, research contributions, and limitations.

Understanding a paper requires more than simply summarizing its content.

IndicAi aims to build a research intelligence system that processes academic papers, extracts traceable evidence, and uses specialized AI investigators to examine the paper from different research perspectives.

The system is designed around one important principle:

**The original research paper remains the source of truth.**

AI-generated findings must remain connected to the original evidence so that conclusions can be checked against their actual sources.

## 2. Core Architecture

![alt text](<LangGraph Research Paper Intelligence Architecture.png>)

The project is divided into two major systems:

### Part 1 — Internal Paper Intelligence System

This system processes an uploaded research paper and prepares structured evidence for specialized investigators.

Current processing pipeline:

1. PDF Ingestion
2. Document Extraction
3. Evidence Building
4. Research Context Preparation
5. Parallel AI Investigation
6. Discovery Record Validation
7. Discovery Record Storage

### Part 2 — User-Facing Research System

Planned for a later development phase.

This system will:

1. Accept a user's research question.
2. Search discovery records produced by the three investigators.
3. Retrieve relevant evidence references.
4. Fetch the corresponding original passages from the paper.
5. Use a final reasoning model to formulate an evidence-grounded answer.

The final reasoning model must verify findings against the original paper rather than blindly trusting investigator-generated descriptions.

## 3. Three Specialized AI Investigators

The internal intelligence system will use three independent research perspectives.

| Investigator | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Investigator 1 — Learning & Comprehension | Understand concepts, definitions, methods, equations, figures, and mechanisms. |
| Investigator 2 — Research Exploration | Identify contributions, research gaps, assumptions, relationships, and future research possibilities. |
| Investigator 3 — Critical Evaluation | Examine claims, supporting evidence, experimental methodology, datasets, evaluation metrics, limitations, and reliability. |

Each investigator will produce structured discovery records with references to the original paper.

These investigators are not intended to produce three ordinary paper summaries. They perform different forms of research investigation.

## 5. Evidence Architecture

The system distinguishes between original evidence and AI-generated discoveries.

### Original Evidence

Contains information extracted directly from the research paper:

- Paper ID
- Evidence ID
- Original page number
- Passage index
- Evidence type
- Original extracted text

Example evidence identifier:

`paper_001:p4:e2`

This represents a passage from page 4 of paper `paper_001`.

### Discovery Records

These will contain structured findings generated by the three investigators.

Each discovery record will maintain references to the original evidence used to support that finding.

**Important:** Discovery records will never replace or overwrite the original evidence.

## 8. Development Philosophy

IndicAi is being developed with a modular and test-oriented engineering approach.

Our development principles:

- Build individual components before connecting the complete graph.
- Keep responsibilities separated between processing nodes.
- Preserve source traceability throughout the pipeline.
- Validate structured data before passing it to subsequent components.
- Use Git version control to preserve meaningful development checkpoints.
- Avoid unnecessary LLM calls for deterministic processing tasks.
- Keep the original research paper as the final authority for evidence verification.

Development workflow:

`Design → Implement → Test → Review → Commit → Push`

## 10. Project Vision

The goal of IndicAi is not to create another tool that simply summarizes academic papers.

The goal is to develop a system that can investigate research papers from multiple perspectives, preserve the evidence behind its findings, and answer research questions with traceable support from the original source.

**Research should be understandable, inspectable, and verifiable.**
